# IEEE 14-bus Pmin-zero, symmetric-Q study results

The 14-bus model has 15 bus records (14 solved buses), 6 generator records (5 online), 22 branch records (20 online), 4 transformers (3 online), and 2 fixed plus 2 switched shunts. The AC OPF and fixed-equipment AC SCOPF solved locally with objective **988.44267507 GOC cost units** using PMSC/PowerModels and Ipopt (`tol=1e-6`, `print_level=0`). The one-iteration fixed-point SCOPF and full three-state master agree within 6.3×10⁻⁷. Generator limits are `Pmin=0` and `Qmin/Qmax=±max(|Qmin_original|, |Qmax_original|)`; original values and margins against both original and study limits are in `generator_solution.csv` and the adjacent case's `generator_limits_original_and_study.csv`. Voltage bounds are 0.90–1.10 p.u.; base voltage spans 1.063515–1.100000 p.u. (bus 6 is at Vmax within solver tolerance), and minimum voltage across states is 1.039597 p.u. Maximum base Rate-A loading is 38.30% on transformer 5–6; maximum contingency Rate-C loading is 29.17%. In the two outage states, generator 4 exceeds Pmax by 0.13382 MW and 0.07636 MW; the PMSC recourse formulation does not enforce generator capability boxes in every contingency. Transformer taps and shunt settings are fixed at source values in every state, with zero movement. Full per-component and state-by-state thresholds and margins are in the CSVs.
