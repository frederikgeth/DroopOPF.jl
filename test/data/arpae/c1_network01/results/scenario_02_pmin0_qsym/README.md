# IEEE 500-bus Pmin-zero, symmetric-Q study results

The 500-bus model has 90 generator records (51 online), 599 branch records (593 online), including 131 transformers, and 17 switched shunts. The AC OPF solved locally at **26,306.79375033**; fixed-equipment AC SCOPF solved in four iterations at **32,839.80407658 GOC cost units**. The full five-state master matches within 4.7×10⁻¹⁰. Generator limits are `Pmin=0` and `Qmin/Qmax=±max(|Qmin_original|, |Qmax_original|)`; original values and margins against both original and study limits are in `generator_solution.csv` and the adjacent case's `generator_limits_original_and_study.csv`. Voltage bounds are 0.90–1.10 p.u.; OPF spans 1.005267–1.100000 p.u., while all SCOPF states reach both voltage bounds within solver tolerance. OPF maximum Rate-A loading is 100.0000% on branch 309; the most-loaded transformer is at 91.86%. Across SCOPF states, the highest Rate-C loadings are 100.0000% on the L_000162 line outage, 99.9935% on the L_000246 line outage, and 100.0000% on the T_000247 transformer outage. The source PMSC recourse formulation allows substantial generator capability violations: the generator outage has 54 P/Q bound violations over 0.0001 MW/MVAr, including Pmax exceeded by 95.89 MW and Qmax by 7.47 MVAr; L_000162 has 60 violations (Pmax excess 1.86 MW, Qmax excess 20.84 MVAr), L_000246 has 62 (1.45 MW, 17.18 MVAr), and T_000247 has 60 (1.45 MW, 17.14 MVAr). See `scopf_generator_scenarios.csv` for every margin. Transformer taps and shunt settings are fixed at source values in every state, with zero movement. Full per-component thresholds and margins are in the CSVs.
