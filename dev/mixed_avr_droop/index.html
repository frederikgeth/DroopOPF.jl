Mixed AVR and volt–var droop studies

Generator(...; s_rated=S) declares a positive apparent-power rating in per-unit on the case system MVA base. Omission (nothing) preserves the historical rectangular capability. An explicit rating adds P²+Q² ≤ S² in each optimized AC state; it never replaces P/Q bounds. The setting survives Case conversion, contingencies, retained islands and study JSON (rated studies use schema 11). PSS/E MBASE is not automatically interpreted as a verified nameplate rating. For capability-clipped droop and individual AVR controls with Q intervals spanning zero, the clipped response plus |P|≤S already enforces the circle algebraically. The builder omits a duplicate circle row in those cases to avoid a dependent saturation Jacobian. Other units retain explicit circle inequalities. Independent replay checks the circle for every rated unit regardless of numerical encoding.

reactive_limits(g,p) returns the intersection of the supplied Q interval and [-sqrt(S²-p²), sqrt(S²-p²)]. AVR releases voltage regulation at these effective limits. A volt–var curve retains its designed reference, slope and deadband, but its delivered response clips to the effective capability. Several AVR units regulating the same location share the same normalized position in their currently available reactive intervals. Their target must be consistent.

This is a steady-state control and capability model. The labels AVR and droop do not instantiate synchronous-machine dynamics, a grid-following PLL, a grid-forming controller, current priority or renewable availability. Detailed inverter capabilities remain P3 work. The circle is an apparent-power limit; it does not impose the voltage-dependent current envelope of a real converter.

Deterministic allocation

mixed_reactive_controls(case; avr_fraction=.30, ratings, voltage_targets) ranks online, positive-Pmax units by descending Pmax and ascending ID. The AVR count rounds to nearest integer, half upward. Remaining online units retain their supplied attached volt–var curves. Offline records stay offline. The caller must provide ratings for every eligible unit and targets for exactly the selected AVR units. No device rating is inferred by this helper.

Explicit mixed assignments can be combined with smooth free droop design in optimize_joint_design, optimize_droop_parameters and optimize_multi_period. Stable control IDs refer to legacy attachments; a selected free curve must match the explicit assignment for that generator. Exact complementarity supports fixed mixed curves and P-dependent saturation; free mixed-curve design currently uses the smooth formulation. The frozen feasibility certificate gate (including rational LP and global graph escalation) explicitly rejects rated cases until its P-dependent graph is extended. This avoids applying an obsolete voltage-only droop hull to clipped capability. Ordinary AC/SCOPF native solves and their physical replay support the ratings. The optional legacy voltage-only droop predictor also returns an unsupported guard for rated cases and routes them to exact assessment.

Multi-period security

optimize_multi_period_security(studies; duration_hours, ramp_limits, angle_limits, droop_controls, ...) couples preventive active dispatch by supplied pu/hour ramp rates and uses one shared designed curve per selected controller. Each period has its own base and declared outage states. Equipment follows the Study selections, bounds, timing and movement budgets. Unselected equipment stays fixed. No initial/terminal dispatch, outage trajectories, storage or commitment are inferred. Normal base bounds and full demand always apply.

  • response=:limit_aware uses the production saturating participation model.
  • response=:s2_proportional explicitly uses the saved S2 proportional equality. It does not silently turn into a saturated response.
  • s2_emergency=true selects the specific S2 comparison policy: outage voltage bounds extended by 0.05 pu, branch MVA limits multiplied by 1.1, DR up to 1% of each positive load, shedding up to 10 MW per outage, combined relief up to 10% per load, and proportional reactive relief. These are declared assumptions, not universal security defaults or transient guarantees.
  • Emergency solves sequentially minimize total shedding, total DR, then duration-weighted base loss energy. Earlier optima are retained within 1e-8 system-base pu in the aggregate objective. These remain local NLP stages.
  • AVR targets are fixed by default. Optional avr_bounds=Dict(id=>(lo,hi)) schedules a preventive target per period, shared across its contingencies; avr_movement_per_hour bounds changes between period starts. This parameter is an explicit study assumption, not a manufacturer ramp specification. Units at one regulated location share one exact target, using intersected bounds. An unscheduled AVR at that location holds the common target fixed.
  • Fixed-base evaluation freezes the common curves, states and equipment, then solves listed outage recourse with a feasibility objective; it does not minimize the relief of each accepted response. It is a security assessment of a candidate, not a replacement for jointly optimizing all required constraints.

Accept only native success and validate_multi_period_security(studies,r).valid. Replay independently reconstructs equipment, load relief, original-network currents, exact controls, apparent limits, response, angles, temporal ramps and loss energy. write_multi_period_security / read_multi_period_security preserve results. For external audit, retain the input Study files and campaign contract as well: a result's stored temporal policy is not an independent specification.

500-bus campaign

The driver is examples/p2_mixed_avr_500.jl. It writes a fresh campaign and does not resume the paused S2 work. The reference has 90 records: 51 online positive Pmax units, hence 15 AVR and 36 droop units; 39 offline records remain offline.

The exploratory ratings are explicitly assumed S=Pmax/0.95, not verified source ratings. Fixed AVR targets use nominal S2 saved generator-bus voltages. Droop bounds retain the calibrated 2–8% full-Q-span slopes, reference ±0.02 pu and fixed zero deadband. The equipment policy retains 131 corrective taps and 11 continuous banks with S2 absolute bounds and no extra movement cap. Settled corrective availability is assumed; device delays and discrete bank recovery are unqualified.

The temporal profile is five one-hour periods at 90/100/105/100/90% demand, scaling both P and Q at every load. Preventive ramps are assumed at 25% of each generator's active capability range per hour. A separate AVR scheduling sensitivity permits ±0.02 pu around the fixed target within normal bus bounds, with ≤0.01 pu/hour movement. The connected 389-outage catalogue and structural island exclusions are reported separately. Security cannot be inferred from a valid base trajectory.

See the campaign report for actual executed stages, accepted results, failed attempts and outstanding qualification. Numerical failure is not an infeasibility proof. Neither this extension nor its base-operation results close P2.

The repository campaign ledger, artifacts/p2_mixed_avr_500_20261022/REPORT.md, distinguishes native AC passes, necessary fixed-dispatch exclusions and numerical leftovers. The 14-bus 125-state normal-limit mixed study passes. The 500-bus accepted base profile is not a qualified security-constrained preventive design. The fixed-curve five-period reference is assembled from native independently optimized periods with verified ramps; its bounded coupled-optimizer retries did not qualify.

Reserve-aware preventive dispatch

Loss-only base dispatch can leave participating generators at Pmax. The optional objective_policy=:reserve_loss inserts a reserve stage after shedding/DR and before loss. For each participating online unit with effective active interval [lo, hi], it imposes Pg + (hi-lo)*h <= hi and maximizes the common h across all periods. The loss stage retains that locally achieved reserve level. objective_policy=:reserve stops after the reserve stage; :loss retains the existing default. This normalized headroom preference is not a generator cost curve, an explicit reserve-delivery guarantee, or an outage-security certificate.

result = optimize_multi_period_security(studies;
    droop_controls=controls,
    objective_policy=:reserve,
    avr_bounds=avr_bounds,
    avr_movement_per_hour=0.01,
    response=:s2_proportional,
    s2_emergency=true)

Reserve stages and their attained values survive JSON round trips. The Julia validator and the independent campaign audit recompute headroom from the physical base states and reject loss polishing that violates the retained reserve level. Numerically zero nonnegative shedding/DR optima are fixed at zero before later stages to avoid a nearly dependent aggregate constraint; failed stricter solves remain failures, not accepted approximations.

Frozen-base assessment may encode different supplied AVR targets in each period, provided every other reactive-assignment field matches. These are fixed inputs to outage evaluation, not new corrective AVR degrees of freedom. Qualify the parent preventive schedule against its bounds and hourly movement limit, then verify that every outage scan uses the same parent targets, curves and base states. Frozen scans do not independently optimize or qualify a new schedule.

The fresh continuation driver is examples/p2_mixed_avr_reserve.jl; its evidence ledger is artifacts/p2_mixed_avr_reserve_20261023/REPORT.md. Bounded joint masters are followed by exhaustive frozen-candidate AC assessment. All 389 connected outages must pass all five periods for full qualification; subset resource objectives do not establish full-catalogue resource optimality.

An optional reserve_generator_outages=[...] dispatch target requires each surviving participant to have room for its share of the named lost generator's entire base output plus reserve_margin_pu (default zero), using the declared surviving participation weights and movement caps. It uses effective active bounds and leaves the equipment, circle, voltage and thermal bounds unchanged. This stricter target assumes no relief for active replacement and does not cover changes in network losses; it is not a replacement for an AC outage solve. The campaign contract records this option, and the independent Python audit checks it separately from physics. Result-only replay cannot recover additional input policies that were not retained with the campaign contract.

objective_policy=:feasibility is available for a separate constrained witness without resource or loss optimization. A feasible shared design is not a resource-optimal design. Keep feasibility, reserve and loss-polishing attempts in separate evidence folders and report which objective actually converged.

For a joint master with corrective equipment, initial_equipment[t] may include scenarios=Dict(:base => (taps=..., shunts=...), :outage => (...)). Each scenario uses its own coherent equipment starting values; omitted scenarios fall back to the period entry. Unknown scenario keys are rejected. This option changes numerical initialization only; it does not add corrective controls or relax movement budgets, absolute bounds, or preventive sharing constraints.

A complete cross-attempt primal warm start can also supply one dictionary per period through initial_avr_targets, initial_relief and initial_balancing_power. Relief entries are (dr=..., shed=...) vectors over the original loads, keyed by outage ID; AVR targets are keyed by scheduled generator ID. Unknown IDs, nonfinite data and inconsistent targets at a shared regulated location are rejected. Individual starts are clipped to variable bounds; all original physical and temporal constraints are still enforced. The initial state does not become a valid operating point merely because it was retained across attempts. Inspect all constraint residuals, including AVR and capability relations, as well as power balance.

P2 policy rerun

The current P2 mixed-control rerun uses examples/p2_mixed_limit_aware.jl. It explicitly selects response=:limit_aware and s2_emergency=false, default capability-range participation, original voltage/thermal bounds, and full load without DR/shedding. The effective-range weights equal the former Pmax weights for this particular zero-Pmin data set; saturation redistribution changes the law.

The selected 131 taps retain their absolute supplied +/-5% envelope and may move at most 0.025 of supplied ratio from each period's preventive base. The 11 banks remain continuous within their legal susceptance envelopes, with a two-section movement budget. Each has one section, so its absolute range is the tighter bound. This is an assumed 120-second settled sensitivity; it does not qualify immediate zero-movement security, manufacturer delays or discrete switching. The mixed 15 AVR/36 droop split, assumed apparent circles, five-hour profile and latest shared curve/AVR scheduling bounds remain in force.

The additional proportional-share reserve guard and S2-specific rational capacity filter are not applied. Historical exact-S2 drivers and sealed results retain that historical policy. Fresh results are written to artifacts/p2_mixed_limit_aware_20261023; the original S2 study stays paused.

The completed scan passes 386/389 connected outages across all five periods, including all 51 generator outages. Lines 21 and 36 and transformer 562 remain numerically unresolved. The base candidate retains 21.580884% minimum upward headroom and no generator reaches Pmax in any base period; five-hour base loss energy is 178.13534899 MWh. Its AVR schedule passes bounds and movement checks, but full security qualification remains open. See the P2 policy report.

Targeted P2 recovery under original limits (2026-10-23)

The targeted recovery found a common five-period design for line 21, line 36 and transformer 562 with no rating, voltage, capability or equipment-budget relaxation. Preventive dispatch, shared droop and the AVR schedule were reoptimized with a feasibility objective. The prior reserve-aware solution supplied initialization; its minimum individual headroom was not retained as a constraint. The selected design has 2.811439% minimum individual headroom and 1083.766 MW aggregate peak Pmax-minus-Pg headroom. The report records the subsequent exhaustive frozen-design validation separately from rejected exploratory solves. This does not establish loss/resource optimality, discrete switching feasibility or transient security.

Final frozen-design validation: 389/389 connected outages accepted across all five periods, with independent Python audit, unchanged-policy identity and current-source Julia replay of all 390 selected result files. The five-period connected steady-state AVR schedule is qualified within this declared scope.
