Selected validation references

These compact references illustrate numerical verification of the implemented steady-state equations and declared study policies. They are not field validation, a guarantee for arbitrary networks, or certification of dynamic behavior. Full research archives remain local. No large network inputs or solver logs are included here.

Transformer optimization: a small, interpretable check

This historical M7.1 sweep compares fixed and optimized continuous taps on a synthetic three-bus system, with phase shifts, shunts and droops fixed. The objective is dispatch deviation, not loss minimization. The fixed / one-free / two-free tap solutions have maximum AC residuals of 2.43e-15 / 2.87e-13 / 5.83e-14 pu. See the equipment report tables for settings, objectives, acceptance tolerances and limitations. The figure is copied unchanged from the archived report; its source path and SHA-256 are in the summary below. A sweep supports this bounded comparison, not global optimality in larger models.

Independent replay of the recovered 500-bus joint model

Each point is the largest residual among the base and outages 21, 36 and 562 at that period. There are 20 states in this selected joint model. Julia and independent Python replay evaluate the saved solution; the plot does not represent every state in the subsequent full catalogue. Its small residuals demonstrate numerical agreement with the equations. Acceptance also checks voltage, thermal and capability limits, AVR behavior, response and equipment policy; those checks cannot be replaced by a residual plot.

Preventive AVR schedule

The schedule is optimized jointly with dispatch and shared droop curves, then frozen across outages within each period. Fifteen generators use AVR and 36 use generic volt-var droop. Maximum hourly target movement is 0.00052050 pu, below the declared 0.01 pu/hour budget. Targets stay within nominal +/-0.02 pu clipped to normal voltage bounds. These are steady-state operating setpoints, not a simulation of excitation dynamics.

Provenance and reproduction

Download the compact evidence summary. It includes the plotted residuals and AVR targets, coverage counts, and SHA-256 identities of the source report, result, replay records and sealed manifest. Source campaign: artifacts/p2_targeted_relaxations_20261023. The hashes identify local originals; they do not substitute for publishing those originals or enable full independent reproduction from this small summary alone.

Regenerate the two new plots from the bundled summary, with Python and matplotlib:

python3 tools/release/plot_validation_references.py

The M7 image is an unchanged historical figure. Full campaign reproduction needs the locally archived inputs and evidence, appropriate solver versions and computational resources. These documentation plots do not rerun optimization or change any sealed result.

Software validation is separate: v0.10.0-rc1 passed 3,596 assertions on Julia 1.10/Linux, 1.12/Linux and 1.12/macOS, plus documentation and clean-install checks. Those are release-time records, not newly executed campaign results.
