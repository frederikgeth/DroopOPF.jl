Supported scope and limitations

DroopOPF solves balanced, positive-sequence steady-state AC models with explicit reactive controls and declared operating/control constraints.

CapabilitySupported modelInterpretation limit
Base OPFAC balance, voltage bounds, generator capability and both-terminal branch limitsLocal nonlinear solutions require independent validation.
Reactive controlFreeQ, FixedQ, AVR and saturated/deadband Volt/VAR curvesThese are equilibrium relations, not dynamic controller models.
Equipment designSelected continuous transformer ratios and supported simple-bank admittancesLegal discrete positions and device movement trajectories need separate checks.
SecurityCoupled base/outage states, limit-aware response and declared equipment recourseAcceptance applies to the stated catalogue; exclusions and unresolved solves remain explicit.
Shared curve designBounded slopes, references and supported deadband widthsMultiple local or weakly identified designs can exist.
Multi-period operationShared controls, period-specific dispatch, durations and declared rampsNo inferred storage, commitment or dynamic transition model.
PSS/E importRAW 33/34/36 parsing, strict supported AC conversion and saved-state replayNative version-specific export evidence and missing ratings must be reported explicitly.
Feasibility qualificationPhysical witnesses and supported independently checked outer certificatesA timeout or failed local solve is not an infeasibility proof.

The base OPF objective is weighted schedule deviation. The base multi-period objective is active loss energy. Optional reserve-aware policies are documented separately; no generator fuel costs or manufacturer limits are invented.

Always declare system bases, ratings, generator availability, reactive modes, control bounds, equipment timing and movement budgets before comparing results. Use validation to check the returned physical state and policy. The API reference documents individual entry points.
