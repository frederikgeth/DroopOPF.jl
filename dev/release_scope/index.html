Release scope — v0.10.0-rc1

This is a software prerelease, not closure of all research milestones.

AreaReleased implementationQualification boundary
S1Base AC OPF and reactive/equipment designDeclared sampled 14/500-bus studies; original data assumptions apply
S2Shared security design and scenario assessmentDeclared 14-bus scope closed; original 500-bus research remains paused
P1/P2Sparse models, validation, initialization and bounded scenario workflowsNo general safe predictive skipping or full-catalogue resource/loss optimality claim
P3 multi-periodShared curves, dispatch ramps, outage recourse and AVR schedulesDeterministic sampled steady states, no storage or dynamic trajectories
P3 parserRAW 33/34/36 and saved-state replayStrict AC subset; native v36 export qualification pending
Mixed controlsAVR and generic volt-var with apparent-power circlesDetailed synchronous-machine and GFL/GFM dynamics deferred

The latest selected mixed 500-bus design uses 15 AVR and 36 droop units, assumed S=Pmax/0.95, five hourly demand factors 90/100/105/100/90%, full load, normal bounds, limit-aware active response and shared designed curves. All 389 connected outages pass independent audits. Corrective controls remain continuous: 131 taps, supplied +/-5% absolute envelope and <=0.025 supplied-ratio movement; 11 banks within legal envelopes and <=two-section movement. The assumed 120-second settled stage is not a verified device delay. AVR targets are preventive within each period and move <=0.01 pu/hour. No physical limits were relaxed for the final recovery. The objective was feasibility, not a certified minimum-loss or maximum-reserve solution.

Deferred: general island balancing, dynamic/small-signal validation, discrete switching and verified device timing, current-limited GFL/GFM models, storage, renewable availability, full coupled resource optimization and broad screening qualification. The earlier full model used roughly 17 GiB RAM; separating saved research files does not change that numerical memory requirement.
